The new customer onboarding platform launched last week after four months of development. Early adoption numbers show that onboarding completion rates increased by 23% compared to the previous system, and average support ticket volume for new users dropped by nearly a third. The engineering team is now focused on addressing a handful of edge cases reported during the rollout, particularly around users who sign up through the mobile app rather than the web portal. Marketing plans to expand the campaign to international markets next quarter, starting with Canada and the United Kingdom. The product team is also exploring whether to add a guided video tutorial as a permanent onboarding feature, based on strong engagement numbers seen from a limited test run. Overall feedback from account managers has been positive, though several have flagged that the platform still needs better documentation for enterprise customers with more complex account structures.
